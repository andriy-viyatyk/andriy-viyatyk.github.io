# Rooftop station

Mounted on the north side of the roof, 1.5 m above the surface. The first week showed evenings
about 3 °C warmer than the airport report. The cause was the brick chimney next to the mast: it
stores heat all day and releases it after sunset. Moving the shield 2 m away from the chimney
fixed it.
