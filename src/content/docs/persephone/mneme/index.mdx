**Mneme** is an optional knowledge base inside Persephone. Point it at folders of Markdown notes and
it indexes them for **full-text** and **meaning-based** search. You browse and edit the notes in
Persephone, and your AI agent reads, searches and maintains the same notes over MCP. Your files on
disk stay the source of truth; the index is derived and can be rebuilt at any time.

## Get started

1. **Turn it on.** Open quick settings (the **•••** button at the top right) and switch **Mneme**
   on. It is off by default, and nothing runs or indexes until you turn it on. The same switch is
   in **Settings**, under **Mneme**.
2. **Download the model.** The Mneme page opens and asks for the embedding model. Click
   **Load model**. It is a one-time download of about 340 MB; until then, search works as
   full-text only.
3. **Add a folder.** Click **+ Add root**, pick a folder of Markdown files and give it a short
   name. Mneme indexes it in the background and keeps the index in sync as files change.
4. **Search.** Click the root to open it. Type a question in your own words and press **Enter**.

## What you get

- **Search by meaning.** **Hybrid** search (the default) matches exact words and meaning, so a
  note is found even when it uses different words. **Text** and **Vector** modes are there too,
  with tag and date filters.
- **A wiki tree.** The sidebar shows the root as a tree: open, create, rename and delete notes and
  folders, drop files in from Windows Explorer, or drag notes between roots.
- **Links that work.** Notes are addressed as `mneme://{root}/{path}`, and links between them open
  in Persephone. Right-click a note and choose **Copy Path** to link to it from anywhere.
- **Memory for your agent.** Mneme serves its own MCP endpoint (`http://127.0.0.1:7700/mcp`) with
  file-like tools (`read`, `write`, `edit`, `glob`, `grep`) plus `search`, `tree`, `timeline` and
  `tags`. Connect Claude Code, Claude Desktop or any MCP client, and the agent keeps notes and
  finds them again across sessions.

## Private by design

- **Local only.** The service runs on your machine and listens on `127.0.0.1`. Indexing and
  search never leave it; the only download is the model.
- **Your files, your folders.** Mneme adds a small `.mneme` folder to each root for its index.
  Removing a root stops indexing and never deletes your notes.
